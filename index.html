Spot Check

A private notebook for the Tenth and Eleventh Step practices described in Alcoholics Anonymous.

Your writing stays on this device

Nothing you write here is held on any server. There is no account, no login, and no sync. Every word is stored by your browser on this device alone, and it is never sent anywhere. Nobody, including whoever made this, can read it.

Which also means it can be lost

Because there is no copy anywhere else, clearing your browser data erases everything. On iPhone, Safari also clears storage for sites you have not opened in about a week. Adding this to your home screen exempts it from that and keeps your entries far longer. You do not have to. Everything works either way.

To add it: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

It works offline

Once it has loaded, it keeps working with no signal at all.

Not an official AA product

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by Alcoholics Anonymous World Services, Inc. It is a personal tool. Short questions are quoted from the book with page citations; the book itself is not reproduced here.
